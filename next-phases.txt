Phase 20 — Production Deployment & Infrastructure

Containerize the complete Company OS and establish the production infrastructure around FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, workers, storage, migrations, configuration, secrets, health checks, and observability. The goal is to move the current verified codebase toward a production-ready deployment architecture.

Phase 21 — Pixel Office Frontend & Real-Time Control Center

Build the full React/Next.js Pixel Office frontend and connect it to the existing backend projections and WebSocket infrastructure. Provide real-time visualization of employees, teams, tasks, meetings, approvals, failures, recovery, company activity, and human intervention.

Phase 22 — Multi-Agent Swarm & Distributed Execution

Expand the Agent Runtime from the current organizational model into scalable multi-agent swarm execution. Introduce worker pools, parallel agent coordination, distributed task execution, resource isolation, workload balancing, and reliable large-scale agent orchestration.

Phase 23 — Autonomous Business Operations

Connect the existing strategy, intelligence, workforce, research, software development, and execution systems into a continuous autonomous operating loop. The company should be able to identify objectives, create plans, allocate resources, execute work, verify outcomes, recover from failures, and continuously replan under governance.

Phase 24 — Product, Customer & Go-to-Market Operations

Introduce the business-facing operational layer for products, customers, customer success, support, opportunities, product roadmaps, launches, feedback, analytics, and governed GTM workflows. This connects autonomous internal operations to actual customer and market operations.

Phase 25 — Multi-Company & Company Simulation

Enable multiple autonomous companies to operate simultaneously within the platform. Introduce company isolation, independent organizational structures, separate strategies, workforces, knowledge, resources, environments, and controlled inter-company interactions.

Phase 26 — Advanced Company Intelligence & Decision Support

Expand the intelligence layer into deeper forecasting, scenario analysis, business health monitoring, opportunity detection, risk prediction, resource optimization, and executive decision support across the entire organization.

Phase 27 — Continuous Organizational Learning & Adaptation

Create a mature organizational learning loop where validated outcomes, failures, experiments, decisions, and operational patterns continuously improve future planning and execution. Learning remains governed and cannot directly modify authoritative policies or architecture.

Phase 28 — Autonomous Innovation & New Venture Creation

Extend the research and innovation system into governed venture discovery, new product concepts, business-model exploration, experimentation, validation, and proposal of new company initiatives.

Phase 29 — Enterprise-Scale Autonomous Company

Integrate the entire platform into a large-scale autonomous organization capable of operating multiple departments, products, teams, projects, research programs, customers, and initiatives continuously while maintaining governance, verification, recovery, security, and human oversight.

Phase 30 — Production Hardening, Security & Reliability

Perform a comprehensive production-hardening cycle across the entire platform: security, performance, scalability, disaster recovery, observability, auditability, failure resilience, data isolation, cost controls, compliance boundaries, and operational reliability.

Phase 31 — Autonomous Company Platform 1.0

Finalize the complete platform as a production-grade autonomous organization operating system, consolidating the architecture, APIs, frontend, deployment, multi-agent execution, company intelligence, business operations, governance, security, and documentation into a coherent 1.0 platform.
